# CRITS catalogue and Jiji feasibility

**Investigation date:** 2026-10-15  
**Status:** No Jiji integration is enabled.

## Sources checked

- CRITS Jiji shop: <https://jiji.ug/shop/crits-ltd-co-ug>
- Jiji public terms: <https://jiji.ug/rules.html>
- Jiji seller FAQ, “How can I sell on Jiji?”: <https://jiji.ug/faq/8>
- Public Jiji pages and targeted searches for seller API, catalogue feed, and export documentation.

## Findings and limits

- The public seller FAQ describes posting ads manually. The public material checked did not identify an official seller API, product feed, or catalogue export guide.
- Jiji terms section 7.16 says: “You will not copy, modify, distribute any other User Content without consent of the respective user.” This is not treated as permission to scrape or reuse listing text, images, or other platform content.
- No seller-account access was available during this investigation, so private account export features were not checked.
- No written integration permission or guidance was requested from Jiji. Whether CRITS may export or reuse its own listing content remains unconfirmed.
- The site does not scrape Jiji, import Jiji listings, or claim to stay synchronised with Jiji.

## Recommended maintenance process

Until CRITS receives written guidance and confirms it has the rights to use each item of content:

1. Add products manually or through the staff CSV import using information approved by CRITS.
2. Upload images CRITS owns or has permission to use.
3. Record the source listing URL and the date staff last checked it when relevant.
4. Import records as drafts. Review them in the staff workspace and publish products individually.
5. Keep service adverts out of the product catalogue unless CRITS approves them for the services page.

If CRITS wants automated synchronisation, first obtain a written answer from Jiji that identifies an approved API, feed, or seller export and the permitted use of its fields and images. Reassess the integration after that answer; do not build a scraper as a substitute.
